# Singapore Business Signals

Independent research on Singapore business conditions using public data.

## Study 01: Early-Warning Indicators for F&B Demand

Do business expectations and visitor arrivals improve forecasts of Singapore's F&B sales volume over the next three months? In this test, a simple zero-growth forecast beat all three pre-specified models.
